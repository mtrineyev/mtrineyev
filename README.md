# Hi, I’m Maksym Trineiev 👋

### Founder & CEO at [HearMe](github.com/HearMeTech)

I am a Python developer and tech founder driven by a deeply personal mission: **to give everyone the power to be heard.**

This journey began with my godson. He has Cerebral Palsy (CP) and loves to communicate, but severe speech and motor impairments leave him unable to speak clearly or even type a text message. Watching his frustration inspired me to build a solution.

My focus is leading **HearMe**: an AI-powered mobile application designed to understand and amplify the voices of those with speech impairments. As the sole founder, I am currently responsible for both our business strategy and the core technical architecture, building a platform that leverages advanced on-device machine learning and a robust cloud infrastructure (GCP & Firebase) to create hyper-personalized speech recognition models.

---

### 🚀 What I'm Focused On

* **Business & Product:** Defining the product roadmap, business strategy, and ensuring we build a tool that truly serves our users' needs.
* **Technology & AI:** Architecting and building the core backend (Python/FastAPI), AI "model factory" (Vertex AI/GCP), and infrastructure (Firebase).
* **User-Centric Design:** Driving development of a tool that provides privacy (on-device processing) and reliability (offline-first) — because communication cannot depend on an internet connection.
